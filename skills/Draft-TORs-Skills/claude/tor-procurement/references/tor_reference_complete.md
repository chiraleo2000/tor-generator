## Quick Summary

### Legal Hierarchy:
1. Act (พ.ร.บ.) - Public Procurement Act 2560
2. Ministerial Rules (กฎกระทรวง) - Budget thresholds, methods, procedures
3. Regulations (ระเบียบ) - Ministry of Finance Regulations 2560
4. Announcements (ประกาศ) - Policy Committee standards
5. Circulars (หนังสือเวียน) - CGD interpretations and guidelines

### Key Circulars for TOR:
- ว.119 - Specification standards
- ว.56 - e-Bidding document conditions
- ว.159 (Mar 2566) - Construction TOR standards
- ว.322 - Continuous lease/hire exceptions
- ว.336 - Construction guidelines
- ว.356 - Bidder qualifications and timelines

### Procurement Methods:
| Method | Condition | Budget |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| e-market (quote) | Items in e-catalog | 500,001-5,000,000 THB |
| e-market (auction) | Items in e-catalog | > 5,000,000 THB |
| e-bidding | Not in e-catalog / technical | > 500,000 THB |
| Price inquiry | No internet area | 500,001-5,000,000 THB |
| Selection | S.56(1) conditions | Any |
| Specific | <= 500,000 or special | <= 500,000 THB |
